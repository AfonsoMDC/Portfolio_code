Seaside Bathing Culture in Ostend

From bathing machine to beach club

Type
School project
School
Don Bosco Sint-Denijs-Westrem, Ghent · 4D/A Graphic Techniques
Parts
5
Made
Style guide, editorial spread, illustration with animation, two websites

The history of bathing culture in Ostend, from the Belle Époque to today. I built a consistent visual world around it: first a style guide, then a spread, a character with animation and two websites, all in the same language.

01

The style guide

Four parts: typography, colour palette, colour variations, visual identity. Bodoni Moda for titles, Lora for running text. Storm Gray, Mist White and Sand Beige form the base; Slate Blue, Ochre Gold and Cream Ivory add to it; Rust Red is the sparing accent.

02

The spread

Large title in Bodoni Moda, a cut-out bathing machine, wavy lines and columns about the birth of the seaside resort, elegance on the beach and the evolution of swimwear. At the bottom: then versus now.

03

Character in motion

A vector illustration of a figure with a black hat and blue apron, with zigzag lines as texture, and a short animation of it.

04

Ostend — City by the Sea

Tourist site with eight places not to miss, a piece about the city and a contact form. HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.

05

Seaside Bathing Culture in Ostend

Storytelling site in the style of the guide, with Story, Evolution and Image archive. HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.
