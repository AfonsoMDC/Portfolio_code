Disco Revisited

Rhythm, light and freedom

Type
School project
School
Don Bosco Sint-Denijs-Westrem, Ghent · 4D/A Graphic Techniques
When
2025
Parts
5
Made
Moodboards, editorial spread, motion design, two websites

Disco as visual culture: big letters, mirror balls, colour and rhythm, starting from the film Saturday Night Fever (1977). First moodboards, then an editorial spread, a short video and two websites — one about disco, one about the Memphis group.

01

Moodboards

Two boards full of colour, shape and mood. Everything that followed was built from them.

02

The spread

‘DISCO’ in outline letters, a yellow-orange subtitle, a violet field with a quote and a cut-out figure with a mirror ball as head. The text runs in columns and boxes around the images: from underground to mainstream, the sound, image and style, the 12-inch single.

03

Disco in motion

Short motion design video after Saturday Night Fever: light, rhythm and colour in motion.

04

From spread to HTML

The spread rebuilt for the web, with yellow subheadings, the video and a box about the 12-inch single. HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.

05

Memphis design

About the Memphis movement, started in Italy in 1981 around Ettore Sottsass: bright colours, geometric shapes, patterns. HTML and CSS, shown here as a screenshot.
