Privacy and rights

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Afonso Matos da Cruz, Ghent, Belgium. Contact: afonsomatos820@gmail.com.

What data does this website collect?

None automatically. The website uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking services and stores nothing in your browser. That is why there is no cookie banner. The hosting provider may keep technical log files, such as your IP address and the time of your visit; this is needed to run the site and keep it secure.

Contact form and email

If you fill in the form, your name, email address, subject and message are sent to my email address via the service Formspree, which acts as an external processor. I only use your details to reply and do not keep them longer than necessary. The same applies if you email me directly.

External links

The typefaces Instrument Serif and Inter Tight are loaded from this website itself; there is no connection to Google Fonts. Instagram and YouTube are plain links: only when you click them do you reach their websites, where their privacy rules apply.

Your rights

You can ask which data I hold about you, have it corrected or erased, or object to its use. Email me for this. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).

If you want something changed or removed, email me. The typefaces Instrument Serif and Inter Tight are licensed under the SIL Open Font License; the licence text is in the lettertypen folder.
