About me

Crossmedia student in Ghent, with a soft spot for typography, colour and details.

My route: first STEM techniques, then Graphic Techniques, now Crossmedia at Don Bosco Sint-Denijs-Westrem. Along the way posters, print, websites, photography and film were added.

I want to understand why a design works, and I test that in school projects and personal work. Besides designing, I play sports, love to travel and follow technology.

What I’m looking for: a place to learn, to work with feedback and to contribute to real projects.
